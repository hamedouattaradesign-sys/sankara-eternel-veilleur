Virtual tour

Turning around the monument

The monument reveals itself as you turn: the portrait appears on two opposite faces only, and the other two give nothing but the skin of the steel. Drag the image, or use the slider and the arrow keys.

SANKARA, L'ÉTERNEL VEILLEUR, Hamed Ouattara, 2026, Corten steel. Thirty views photographed at the Parco Thomas Sankara, Rome, October 2026, turning around the work by one twelfth of a turn at each step.

How to look

  • The two portrait faces. The face does not exist in relief: it is made of the voids cut through the plate. Stop at 0 and at 180 degrees.
  • The two texture faces. A quarter turn from there, no cuts at all: only the hemispherical bosses, four columns by twelve rows.
  • The corners. Between the two, you see the cut and the skin at once, and the six millimetres of the steel.

By keyboard: reach the slider with the tab key, then turn with the left and right arrows.

Built for slow connections

The rotation uses no three-dimensional model, no WebGL and no outside library. It is a sequence of still images: it works on old phones as on recent browsers, and downloads only what it shows.

If your device reports a slow connection or data saver mode, only one view in three is loaded. The rest comes only when you ask for it, with the “load the full rotation” button.
