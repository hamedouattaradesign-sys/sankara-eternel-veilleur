Project diary

A monument in the making

From the request for a work to its installation in a park in Rome. This page follows the stages as they come, and will stay open after the unveiling.

  1. Saturday 24 October 2026

    The unveiling, at the Parco Thomas Sankara

    The monument was unveiled at the Parco Thomas Sankara, Via Ugo della Seta, in the III Municipio of Rome, on Saturday 24 October 2026, at the official opening ceremony.

    The unveiling fell within the Days of Homage to Comrade President Thomas Sankara, from 23 to 25 October, organised by the Embassy of Burkina Faso to Italy under the patronage of Comrade Pingdwendé Gilbert Ouédraogo, Minister of Communication, Culture, Arts and Tourism, with delegates of the Haut Conseil des Burkinabè de l'Extérieur taking part.

    The monument is now freely open to view, every day.

  2. On the eve of the unveiling — Parco Thomas Sankara

    The park gets ready

    The monument stands on its plinth, still covered, hours from the unveiling. Around it the park is being fitted out: the Burkina Faso display banners are up, site chains mark out the approach, and the stage for the Days has been built.

    The author of the work presents the monument to Comrade Pingdwendé Gilbert Ouédraogo, Minister of Communication, Culture, Arts and Tourism, who has come to Rome for the Days of tribute.

  3. October 2026 — Rome

    The monument is in place

    The stepped reinforced concrete base was cast on site, in the park. The steel prism was set on it and sealed. The monument now stands at the Parco Thomas Sankara. Its siting was settled on the spot, according to the park's surroundings and the ways people come to it.

    Hamed Ouattara is in Rome for the Days of Homage.

  4. 2026 — Bologna

    The making, finished

    The plates were laser cut and the bosses pressed by specialist workshops, from the files sent from Ouagadougou. The steel has taken its first oxidation: the monument is ready to leave for Rome.

    The name ISIDORE NOEL THOMAS SANKARA 1949-1987 and the author's signature are cut into the steel on the same principle as the portrait.

  5. 17 September 2025 — Rome

    The park receives its name

    The III Municipio of Rome names the green space on Via Ugo della Seta, in the Montesacro district: Parco Thomas Sankara. The commemorative plaque is unveiled in the presence of Comrade Cyrille Ganou/Badolo, Ambassador of Burkina Faso to Italy, and of representatives of the City of Rome.

  6. Ouagadougou

    The design

    The work was conceived in full at the Studio Hamed Ouattara, from the sculptural decision through to the cutting files: the choice of a hollow prism rather than a statue, the portrait in negative space, the skin of bosses, and the count of the elements settled after research into the chronology of the Democratic and Popular Revolution.

    A request, and a park

    Comrade Madi Sakandé, of the Burkinabè diaspora in Italy, gives a work to the benefit of the Burkinabè State for the Parco Thomas Sankara in Rome. He asks Hamed Ouattara for a work, and not a statue.
