The Bologna workshop

The plates were laser cut and the bosses pressed by specialist workshops. The assembly, the welding and the finishing were done by the artist himself.

The drawing of the portrait face

The fabrication document sent to the workshops, dimensioned to the millimetre: the top row of four bosses, the face and the name block cut clean through, the twenty-four bosses of the lower part, and the artist's signature.

After the installation

As soon as the monument stands at the Parco Thomas Sankara, this gallery will grow with photographs of the work in place, in the park, at every hour of the day. The site will then open the virtual tour: a complete rotation around the monument, for those who cannot come to Rome.
